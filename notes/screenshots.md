# User-guide screenshots

Screenshots are split by platform and locale. Keep the same file names in every locale folder:

- `library.png`
- `viewer.png`
- `settings.png`

Android captures were taken from the connected Galaxy tablet in `ko`, `en`, `ja`, `es`, `de`, `fr`, and `pt-BR`.
iOS/iPadOS captures are deferred until the iOS app is stable. When documentation work resumes,
capture them on the Mac and place them in `docs/ScoreCue/assets/screenshots/ios/<locale>/`.
